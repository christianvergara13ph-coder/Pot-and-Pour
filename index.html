Our little story

Where your drink meets your plant.

Pot & Pour was imagined as a cozy little space for people who love two simple things: a good drink and something green to take home.

Whether you're stopping by for a creamy matcha, looking for a tiny plant for your desk, or simply want a peaceful corner to hang out, Pot & Pour is your little pause in the middle of the day.

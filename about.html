Our Story

Where a good drink meets a little green.

Pot & Pour is a cozy coffee and plant shop concept made for people who enjoy matcha, small indoor plants, and a relaxing place to slow down.

The website brings the two sides of the shop together so visitors can explore the menu, discover plants, learn about the shop, and place a simple order.
